## Full description

Business Buddy is a simple business companion for plumbers, salons, mechanics, and other small service businesses in South Africa.

It is **not** a full accounting package. It helps you:

• Keep customers and job history in one place  
• Send professional PDF invoices  
• Record cash, EFT, and card payments  
• Track expenses and estimated profit  
• See today’s work and money on a clear dashboard  

**Built for real days on the tools**

Create a customer, book a job, finish the work, invoice, and get paid — without wrestling a desktop accounting system on your phone.

**Offline-friendly**

Keep working when signal drops. Changes sync when you’re back online.

**Private by design**

We use anonymous crash and usage analytics to improve the app. We do not send customer names, phone numbers, or invoice amounts to analytics.

Pricing (planned): R99 / R199 / R299 per month with a 14-day trial.
