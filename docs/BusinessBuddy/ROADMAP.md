# Product Roadmap

---

# Near-Term Product Vision (Version 1)

A lightweight business companion where owners can manage:

* Customers
* Bookings / Jobs
* Quotes
* Invoices
* Dashboard
* Payments
* Simple Expenses
* Notes / Timeline
* PDF exports
* Reports

Nothing fancy. Just incredibly fast.

# Explicitly Deferred (Do Not Build First)

* Full accounting / SARS compliance
* Payroll
* Bank reconciliation
* Balance sheets
* Barcode scanning
* Multi-branch enterprises
* Complex inventory systems
* Multi-user admin portals beyond basic roles

---

# Go-to-Market Sequence

1. Choose one profession first (e.g. plumbers or salons)
2. Build smallest usable Version 1
3. Put it in front of 5 real business owners
4. Learn which one feature they demand next
5. Expand only after one group loves it

---

# Pricing Target

* Free trial: 14 days
* Basic: R99/month
* Pro: R199/month
* Premium: R299/month

Success example:
50 customers at R199/month ≈ R9,950/month recurring before expenses.
