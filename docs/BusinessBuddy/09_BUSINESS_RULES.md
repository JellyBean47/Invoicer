# Purpose

This document defines every business rule used by Business Buddy.

Business rules determine how data behaves.

These rules are mandatory.

The application must never violate these rules.

---

# General Rules

Business Buddy exists to reduce mistakes.

Whenever possible,
the application should perform calculations automatically.

The user provides information.
The software performs the mathematics.

---

# Customer Rules

Every customer belongs to exactly one business.

Phone numbers must be unique within a business.

Customers may exist without
Email
Company
Address
Notes

Customers cannot exist without
Name
Phone Number

---

Archived Customers

Archived customers remain
Searchable
Reportable
Visible in history

Invoices remain linked.
Payments remain linked.
Jobs remain linked.

Archived customers cannot receive
New Jobs
New Quotes
New Invoices
until restored.

---

# Job Rules

Every job belongs to exactly one customer.
Every job belongs to exactly one business.

A job cannot exist without
Customer
Title
Status

Jobs follow this lifecycle

Draft
↓
Scheduled
↓
In Progress
↓
Completed
or
Cancelled

Completed jobs cannot become Draft.
Cancelled jobs remain visible.
Completed jobs may generate invoices.
Cancelled jobs cannot.

---

# Quote Rules

Quotes are estimates.
Quotes do not affect reports.
Quotes do not affect revenue.
Quotes may expire.
Accepted quotes may convert into jobs.
Rejected quotes remain in history.
Quote numbers never duplicate.

---

# Invoice Rules

Invoices are financial records.
Invoices become immutable once finalized.

Draft invoices may be edited.
Final invoices may not.

Invoice numbers
Never duplicate.
Never change.
Never recycle.

Invoices cannot exist without
Customer
Issue Date
At least one invoice item
Grand Total

---

Invoice Status Lifecycle

Draft
↓
Final
↓
Partially Paid
↓
Paid
or
Overdue

Cancelled only allowed before Final.

---

# Invoice Item Rules

Each invoice item requires

Description
Quantity
Unit Price
Line Total

Line Total
Always calculated.
Never manually entered.

Quantity
Greater than zero.

Unit Price
Cannot be negative.

---

# Labour Rules

Labour behaves exactly like invoice items.

Examples

Installation
Consultation
Call-out Fee
Maintenance

Each labour item uses

Quantity
Unit Price
Automatic Total

---

# Discount Rules

Discounts optional.
Discount cannot exceed subtotal.
Discount cannot create negative totals.
Discount applied before tax.

Formula

Subtotal
↓
Discount
↓
Tax
↓
Grand Total

---

# Tax Rules

Business default tax stored in Settings.
Invoices may override tax percentage.

Tax percentage
0
to
100

Tax Amount
Always calculated.
Never manually entered.

Tax always calculated after discounts.

---

Every monetary field stored as integer cents.

R15.99

1599

Allowed
Addition
Subtraction
Multiplication

Not Allowed
Floating Point

Decimal Storage
Manual Totals

# Payment Rules

Payments belong to exactly one invoice.
Payments belong to exactly one customer.
Payments belong to exactly one business.

Payment amount
Must be positive.
Cannot exceed outstanding balance.

Invoice automatically updates.
Outstanding balance automatically recalculated.

Invoice becomes
Paid
when outstanding reaches zero.

---

# Expense Rules

Expenses are simple operational costs, not full accounting.

Expenses belong to exactly one business.
Expense amount must be positive.
Expenses use integer cents.
Estimated Profit = Revenue − Expenses.
Expenses never modify invoices or payments.

---

# Report Rules

Reports generated only from
Invoices
Payments
Expenses

Never from quotes.
Never from jobs (except job statistics).

Reports are read-only.
Users cannot edit reports.

---

# Search Rules

Search ignores
Uppercase
Lowercase
Extra spaces
Phone formatting

Search updates while typing.

Sequential.
Unique.

# Notification Rules

Only useful notifications.
Never spam.
Maximum

# Offline Rules

All operations added to sync queue.

Conflicts resolved according to documented rules.

---

# Non-Negotiable Rules

These rules may never be violated.

Invoices cannot silently change after finalization.
Payments cannot exceed invoice balances.
Money is never stored using floating-point values.
Users never type calculated totals.
Financial reports are generated from source records.
Every action affecting money is logged.
Business data is isolated.
Nothing is permanently deleted without an explicit retention policy.

The software performs the calculations.
The user performs the business.

---

# Guiding Principle

Business Buddy is trusted because it is predictable.

Every calculation produces the same result.
Every report can be verified.
Every invoice can be traced.
Every payment has a history.

If the owner trusts the numbers,
they will trust the application.

Trust is the product.
