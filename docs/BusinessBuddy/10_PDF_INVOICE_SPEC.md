# Purpose

This document defines the official Business Buddy invoice layout.

Every PDF invoice generated by the application must follow this specification.

Invoices are legal business documents and must remain professional, readable and consistent.

Do not generate the PDF as a screenshot of the UI.
Generate from structured data using a dedicated PDF template so it always looks consistent regardless of phone size.

# Invoice Layout

The invoice consists of the following sections:

1. Header
2. Business Information
3. Customer Information
4. Invoice Details
5. Item Table
6. Totals
7. Payment Information
8. Notes
9. Footer

---

Displays

Business Logo (optional)
Business Name
Invoice Title

Header must be visually prominent.

# Labour

Labour appears inside the same table as products.

Example

PVC Pipe
2
R120.00
R240.00

Labour
3 Hours
R350.00
R1050.00

No separate labour section.
Everything is an invoice line item.

---

# Table Rules

Descriptions wrap automatically.
Currency values right aligned.
Header row repeated if multiple pages.
Rows numbered automatically.
Empty rows not displayed.

# Payment QR Codes (Optional)

If the business owner enters banking details (or future payment providers), the invoice may include a QR code customers can scan to pay.

* Customer receives invoice on WhatsApp
* Opens PDF
* Scans QR code
* Pays immediately
* Owner records payment (or future auto-match)

Optional, but reduces friction at getting paid.

# Branding

Business Logo optional.
Business colors not applied to financial totals.
Financial information remains black for printing clarity.
Minimal accent color permitted in headers.

---

# Multi-Page Rules

Header repeats.
Table header repeats.
Footer appears on every page.
Totals appear only on final page.
No invoice item split across pages if avoidable.
